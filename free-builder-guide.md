# Which free AI app builder should I pick?

A one-page guide from FreeTierWatch. Figures checked **9 Nov 2026** against each tool's official pricing page. £ prices use 1 USD = 0.7484 GBP (mid-market that day), except where Google publishes its own UK price.

There is no single "best" free plan. Pick by what you need to do this week, then check whether the free plan lets you put something live. Free plans change often — the live table is at [freetierwatch.co.uk](https://freetierwatch.co.uk).

## First: what are you building?

**If you want a quick web app demo with AI doing most of the building**

- **Bolt.new** — free plan: 300K tokens/day and 1M tokens/month, with Bolt branding. Cheapest paid: Pro at $25 (about £18.71)/month.
- **Lovable** — free plan: 5 build credits/day (max 30/month) plus 20 Cloud credits/month. Cheapest paid: Pro at $25 (about £18.71)/month.
- **v0** — free plan: 7 messages/day; deploy to Vercel. Cheapest paid: Plus at $30 (about £22.45)/user/month.
- **Base44** — free plan: 25 message credits + 100 integration credits/month, up to 5 apps. Cheapest paid: Starter at $16 (about £11.97)/month billed annually.
- **Rocket** — free plan: 20 free credits, one-time (they never expire). Cheapest paid: Pro at $25 (about £18.71)/month for 100 credits/month.
- **Emergent** — free plan: 10 credits/month, web and mobile apps. Cheapest paid: Standard at $20 (about £14.97)/month ($17 billed annually).

**If you need a proper no-code app platform (workflows, database, internal tools)**

- **Softr** — free plan: 5 AI credits/month, unlimited apps, 5K database records. Cheapest paid: Basic at $19 (about £14.22)/month billed yearly ($25 billed monthly).
- **Glide** — free plan: 10 projects, limited credits, 2 team members. Cheapest paid: Basic (GlideOS) at $25 (about £18.71)/month.
- **Bubble** — free plan: 500 AI credits/month and 50K workload units/month, **development version only (no live app)**. Cheapest paid: Starter (web) at $39 (about £29.19)/month, or $32 billed annually. You need Starter to launch a live app.
- **Framer** — free plan: 500 AI credits to try, free Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth (free plan is for non-commercial use). Cheapest paid: Basic at $10 (about £7.48)/month with yearly billing.

**If you want an AI coding app (IDE) rather than a hosted app builder**

- **Google Antigravity** — free Individual plan: a variety of agent models, unlimited Tab completions and Command requests, basic weekly rate limits. Higher limits with Google AI Pro at Google's UK price of £18.99/month.
- **Cursor** — free plan: limited agent and chat requests. Cheapest paid: Plus (Grok models only) at $8 (about £5.99)/month; Premium is $30/month.
- **Windsurf** (now Devin Desktop) — free plan: light agent quota, unlimited Tab completions and inline edits. Cheapest paid: Pro at $20 (about £14.97)/month.
- **GitHub Copilot** — free plan: 2,000 code completions/month, limited chat and agent use, entry-level models. Cheapest paid: Pro at $10 (about £7.48)/month.
- **Kiro** (AWS) — free plan: 50 credits/month. Cheapest paid: Pro at $20 (about £14.97)/month for 1,000 credits.
- **Google AI Studio** — Build mode free with a modest daily quota (resets daily); web and Android apps. Higher limits with Google AI Pro at £18.99/month (UK price).
- **Replit** — free Starter: daily Agent credits (monthly cap), monthly cloud credits, Lite build only, **1 published app (link expires after 30 days)**, Replit badge. Cheapest paid: Core at $20 (about £14.97)/month ($18 billed annually). Full build, Plan Mode and connectors need Core.

## Which free plans let you publish?

This is the question that catches people out. From the free-plan wording we track:

- **Bubble free does not let you launch a live app.** The free plan is development version only. Starter (about £29.19/month, or $32 billed annually) is the cheapest paid plan that launches a live web app.
- **Replit free lets you publish one app**, but the published link expires after 30 days and carries a Replit badge. Lite build only on free; full build needs Core.
- **Framer free** includes a free Framer domain and 1 GB bandwidth, and is for non-commercial use.
- **Softr free** allows unlimited apps (with the free limits above).
- **Base44 free** allows up to 5 apps.
- **Glide free** allows 10 projects.
- **v0 free** includes deploy to Vercel.
- For **Lovable, Bolt.new, Emergent, Rocket** and the coding tools, the free row we track is about credits, tokens or quotas — not a separate "you may / may not publish" line. Always read the official pricing page (linked from each row on FreeTierWatch) before you rely on a free live URL or a custom domain.

**Practical tip:** before you invest a week of building, ask: "Can I put this in front of a real user on the free plan, and for how long?" If the answer is no (Bubble) or only for 30 days (Replit free publish), plan the paid step — or pick a tool whose free row matches how you want to launch.
