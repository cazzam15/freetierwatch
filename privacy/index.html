Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This explains what personal data freetierwatch.co.uk collects and how it's used. The site has no accounts and no adverts, and it doesn't set any cookies of its own, so it collects very little.

Who's responsible

The site is run by C. Morrison in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the information below. Contact: hello@freetierwatch.co.uk.

Newsletter sign-up

If you subscribe using the sign-up form, your email address is stored by beehiiv, the newsletter service, and used only to send FreeTierWatch emails (price-change alerts and the cheat sheet). The form is loaded from beehiiv, so beehiiv's own privacy policy applies to it. beehiiv may record whether emails are opened and links are clicked. The legal basis is your consent. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and your address is kept until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be deleted.

Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't set cookies or follow you across other sites. It only records things like which page was viewed, the site you came from, and your browser and country. The legal basis is the site's legitimate interest in knowing which pages are useful. There's no Google Analytics.

Links to other sites

Links to the tools, the Slack community, Instagram, X and GitHub take you to other sites, where their own privacy policies and cookies apply. Some tool links are affiliate links (marked as sponsored in the page code): once you click through, the tool or its affiliate network may use cookies to record that you came from FreeTierWatch.

Who else handles data

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and keeps standard web request logs, including your IP address.
  • beehiiv stores newsletter subscribers' email addresses.
  • Cloudflare processes the visit statistics above and forwards emails sent to hello@freetierwatch.co.uk.

Some of these providers are in the United States. Those transfers are covered by the UK's approved safeguards (the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the UK-US data bridge where it applies). Your data is never sold.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing hello@freetierwatch.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

FreeTierWatch is independent and not affiliated with any tool listed.
