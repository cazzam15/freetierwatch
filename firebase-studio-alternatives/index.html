Firebase Studio is closing: free alternatives (Nov 2026)

Google is closing Firebase Studio. It shuts on 22 March 2027, and since 22 June 2026 nobody can sign up or create a new workspace. If you already have workspaces, you can keep working in them until the sunset date. Apps you already deployed to Firebase keep running, and Firestore, Authentication and App Hosting are not affected. Source: Google's Firebase Studio docs.

Google points people to Google AI Studio or Google Antigravity. Below are those two plus six other tools with a free plan, from our tracker.

FAQ

When does Firebase Studio close?

Google says Firebase Studio is sunsetting on 22 March 2027. New workspace creation and sign-ups have been disabled since 22 June 2026. Existing workspaces keep working until the sunset date.

What happens to apps I already deployed?

Google says apps already deployed to Firebase keep running after the sunset date, and core Firebase products such as Firestore, Authentication and App Hosting are not affected.

Where does Google suggest moving?

Google suggests Google AI Studio for web-based, prompt-based prototyping, or Google Antigravity for a local, code-first setup.

Is Google AI Studio free?

Yes, Google AI Studio has a free plan: Build mode free with a modest daily quota (resets daily); web and Android apps. The cheapest paid plan is Google AI Pro, monthly (UK price £18.99) at $19.99 (about £18.99) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.

Is Google Antigravity free?

Yes, Google Antigravity has a free plan: Individual plan $0: a variety of agent models, unlimited Tab completions and Command requests, basic weekly rate limits. The cheapest paid plan is Google AI Pro, monthly (UK price £18.99) at $19.99 (about £18.99) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.

Is Bolt.new free?

Yes, Bolt.new has a free plan: 300K tokens/day, 1M tokens/month; Bolt branding. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, billed monthly at $25 (about £18.92) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.

Is Lovable free?

Yes, Lovable has a free plan: 5 build credits/day (max 30/month) + 20 Cloud credits/month. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, monthly at $25 (about £18.92) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.

Is Replit free?

Yes, Replit has a free plan: Starter: daily Agent credits (monthly cap), monthly cloud credits, Lite build only, 1 published app (link expires after 30 days), Replit badge. The cheapest paid plan is Core, monthly ($18 billed annually) at $20 (about £15.13) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.

Is Cursor free?

Yes, Cursor has a free plan: Limited agent and chat requests. The cheapest paid plan is Plus (Grok models only) at $8 (about £6.05) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.

Is GitHub Copilot free?

Yes, GitHub Copilot has a free plan: 2,000 code completions/month, limited chat and agent use, entry-level models. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, per month at $10 (about £7.57) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.

Is Kiro free?

Yes, Kiro has a free plan: 50 credits/month, open-weight models and Claude Sonnet 4.5 (with limits). The cheapest paid plan is Pro, per user/month, 1,000 credits at $20 (about £15.13) a month. Checked 2026-11-16.
