FAQ

Which AI app builder has the best free plan?

It depends what you are building. As of 2026-10-19: Lovable: 5 build credits/day (max 30/month) + 20 Cloud credits/month; Bolt.new: 300K tokens/day, 1M tokens/month; Bolt branding; v0: 7 messages/day; deploy to Vercel; Replit: Starter: daily Agent credits (monthly cap), monthly cloud credits, Lite build only, 1 published app (link expires after 30 days), Replit badge; Base44: 25 message credits + 100 integration credits/month, up to 5 apps; Cursor: Limited agent and chat requests; Windsurf: Light agent quota, unlimited Tab completions and inline edits; Framer: 500 AI credits to try, free Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth; Softr: 5 AI credits/month, unlimited apps, 5K database records; Glide: 10 projects, limited credits, 2 team members; Google AI Studio: Build mode free with a modest daily quota (resets daily); web and Android apps; Emergent: 10 credits/month, web and mobile apps. Compare them in the table above.

Which AI app builder is cheapest to upgrade?

Cursor has the lowest cheapest paid plan in our table: Plus (Grok models only) at $8 (about £6.00) a month. Note: Plus includes Grok models only; Premium is $30/mo.

Is Lovable free?

Yes, Lovable has a free plan: 5 build credits/day (max 30/month) + 20 Cloud credits/month. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, monthly at $25 (about £18.76) a month. Checked 2026-10-19.

Is Bolt.new free?

Yes, Bolt.new has a free plan: 300K tokens/day, 1M tokens/month; Bolt branding. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, billed monthly at $25 (about £18.76) a month. Checked 2026-10-19.

Is Replit free?

Yes, Replit has a free plan: Starter: daily Agent credits (monthly cap), monthly cloud credits, Lite build only, 1 published app (link expires after 30 days), Replit badge. The cheapest paid plan is Core, monthly ($18 billed annually) at $20 (about £15.01) a month. Checked 2026-10-19.
