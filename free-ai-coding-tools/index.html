FAQ

Which free AI coding tool is cheapest to upgrade?

Of these, Cursor has the cheapest paid plan: Plus (Grok models only) at $8 (about £5.99) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.

Is Cursor free?

Yes, Cursor has a free plan: Limited agent and chat requests. The cheapest paid plan is Plus (Grok models only) at $8 (about £5.99) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.

Is Windsurf free?

Yes, Windsurf has a free plan: Light agent quota, unlimited Tab completions and inline edits. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, monthly at $20 (about £14.97) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.

Is GitHub Copilot free?

Yes, GitHub Copilot has a free plan: 2,000 code completions/month, limited chat and agent use, entry-level models. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, per month at $10 (about £7.48) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.

Is Kiro free?

Yes, Kiro has a free plan: 50 credits/month, open-weight models and Claude Sonnet 4.5 (with limits). The cheapest paid plan is Pro, per user/month, 1,000 credits at $20 (about £14.97) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.

Is Google Antigravity free?

Yes, Google Antigravity has a free plan: Individual plan $0: a variety of agent models, unlimited Tab completions and Command requests, basic weekly rate limits. The cheapest paid plan is Google AI Pro, monthly (UK price £18.99) at $19.99 (about £18.99) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.
