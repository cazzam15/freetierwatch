FAQ

Is Lovable free?

Yes, Lovable has a free plan: 5 build credits/day (max 30/month) + 20 Cloud credits/month. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, monthly at $25 (about £18.71) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.

Is Bolt.new free?

Yes, Bolt.new has a free plan: 300K tokens/day, 1M tokens/month; Bolt branding. The cheapest paid plan is Pro, billed monthly at $25 (about £18.71) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.

Is Replit free?

Yes, Replit has a free plan: Starter: daily Agent credits (monthly cap), monthly cloud credits, Lite build only, 1 published app (link expires after 30 days), Replit badge. The cheapest paid plan is Core, monthly ($18 billed annually) at $20 (about £14.97) a month. Checked 2026-11-09.
