FAQ

Is Google Antigravity free?

Yes, Google Antigravity has a free plan: Individual plan $0: app, CLI and IDE extensions, baseline agent quota refreshed weekly; Gemini 3.8 Flash (Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-OSS-120b listed until 30 Nov 2026). The cheapest paid plan is Google AI Pro, monthly (UK price £18.99) at $19.99 (about £18.99) a month. Checked 2026-11-23.

How much is Google Antigravity in pounds?

Google Antigravity's cheapest paid plan is Google AI Pro, monthly (UK price £18.99) at $19.99 (about £18.99) a month. £18.99 is Google's own UK price, not a conversion. Checked 2026-11-23.

What do you lose on the free plan on 30 November 2026?

Google's Antigravity pricing page says Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-OSS-120b will be removed from the free Individual plan on 30 November 2026. Gemini 3.8 Flash stays listed. Checked 23 Nov 2026.

Is there a separate Antigravity subscription?

No. Higher Antigravity limits come with a Google AI plan: Google AI Pro (£18.99/month in the UK) or Google AI Ultra (from £79.99/month in the UK).

Can I use Antigravity Pro with a work Google account?

No. Google says Google AI Pro and Ultra plans require a personal Google Account, so Workspace work accounts can't use them. Work accounts can pay as you go through Google Cloud or use Gemini Enterprise instead.
